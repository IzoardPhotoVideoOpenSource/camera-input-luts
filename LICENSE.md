## Origin of the transformations

Some LUTs in this repository are derived from technical colour transformations published by camera manufacturers.

For example, the OPPO O-Log and O-Log 2 LUTs were generated using OPPO's published DCTL transformations, followed by conversion to DaVinci Wide Gamut / DaVinci Intermediate.

The resulting `.cube` files contain numerical colour-transformation data rather than copies of the manufacturers' original DCTL source code.

The original manufacturers retain any applicable intellectual-property rights in their materials and transformations.

IzoardPhotoVideo has requested clarification of the redistribution permissions applicable to these derived LUTs. This notice does not imply that the manufacturers have yet formally authorised their distribution.

These LUTs are independently prepared resources and are not official manufacturer products or endorsed by their manufacturers.
